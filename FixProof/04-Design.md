# 04 — Design

**Mission:** Make security legible to people who know nothing about security. A
FixProof finding and fix should feel as clear and calm as "update available —
install," not a 100-line scanner dump.

## What this department owns
- Product UX — especially the report → fix → approve → confirmed flow.
- The visual language of **trust** (a security product must *feel* safe and
  credible).
- Brand identity: name, logo, colors, type (with Marketing on voice).
- The free-score experience that converts anxiety into action.

## Design principles
1. **Plain language over jargon** — every finding as "what it is, why it matters
   to *you*, what we did about it."
2. **Calm, not alarmist** — we reduce fear, we don't sell it.
3. **One clear next action** per screen — usually "review and approve this fix."
4. **Evidence builds trust** — show the harmless proof simply so a non-expert
   believes it without understanding the exploit.
5. **The merge moment is sacred** — the human-approval checkpoint must be
   obvious, understandable, reversible.

## Current priorities (Phase 0)
1. Design the **findings + fix report** a non-technical owner understands (even
   delivered semi-manually at first).
2. Sketch the **free security score** screen — the funnel hook.
3. Propose **brand basics**: name lockup, palette, one-line identity.
