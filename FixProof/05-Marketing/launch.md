# Launch sequence (later — gated on validation)

**Do not run this until the Phase-0 gate is cleared** (≥5 paying + safe-fix rate
≥80%, per `00-Company.md`). Launching before the wedge is proven burns the one
launch you get. In Phase 0, marketing stays quiet and targeted; this is for when
you have proof.

## Pre-launch (while validating)
- Build-in-public thread as you run the 20 tests (aggregate, consented, honest).
- Collect testimonials + consented before/after fixes from the free batch.
- Lock brand, name, handles, landing page, and the safe-fix-rate number.
- Build a small waitlist from content + outreach interest.

## Launch assets
- The flagship explainer ("5 holes AI-built apps ship with") as the anchor.
- A 60–90s demo video: find → prove → fix → confirmed, on an authorized app.
- The safe-fix-rate stat, front and center ("X% of our fixes closed the hole
  without breaking the app, across N real apps").
- 3 short testimonials from free-batch builders.

## Launch moments (sequence, not all at once)
1. **Waitlist → early access** email: "You can run it yourself now."
2. **Build-in-public recap:** "We tested N AI-built apps. Here's what we learned —
   and what we built." Post on X/LinkedIn.
3. **Community launch:** the maker/indie launch platforms, builder subreddits and
   Discords where your user already is.
4. **Creator seeding:** give the "I built an app with AI" creators free runs; let
   the proof speak.

## Messaging at launch
Lead with **relief and proof**, not fear. "Thousands of apps ship unsafe. Here's
the one that checks and fixes yours — with your approval." Back every claim with
the real number.

## What success looks like
Not a traffic spike — **qualified authorized tests in the pipeline and a rising
paid-fix conversion.** Judge the launch by pipeline and safe-fix rate, the same
numbers that run the company.

## The bigger play to tee up
Start conversations with the **AI app-builder platforms** about FixProof as their
built-in security step. The launch proof (safe-fix rate + testimonials) is your
opening argument for that distribution deal — the real path to scale.
