# 05 — Marketing

**Mission:** Reach builders shipping AI-made apps, name the fear they already
feel, and convert it into a free security score — then a paying customer. Never
sell on panic; the **demonstrated fix** is the credibility.

## Phase 0 job: marketing serves validation, not growth
The entire near-term purpose of marketing is to **help GTM (dept 06) land 20
authorized tests and find 5 who pay.** Not followers, not traffic — authorized
app owners in the pipeline.

## What's in this folder
| File | What it is | Use it to |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| `positioning.md` | Who we are, who we're for, why we're different | Anchor every message |
| `messaging.md` | Taglines, one-liners, value props, objection handling | Write anything on-brand |
| `outreach.md` | Cold email + DM templates (the 20-test engine) | Fill the Phase-0 pipeline |
| `landing-page.md` | Copy for the free-score → verified-fix funnel | Stand up a one-page site |
| `content-plan.md` | Channels + first content pieces | Build authority with builders |
| `launch.md` | The public launch sequence (later) | Launch when the wedge is proven |

## Non-negotiables (from Legal, dept 08)
- Every demonstration or case study uses **only authorized, consented** material.
  Never show a real customer's unfixed holes.
- No fear-mongering, no fake urgency, no invented stats or "share of voice."
- Claims about our quality cite the real **safe-fix rate**, nothing we can't back.

## How it connects
Funnel definition from **Product** (01), brand from **Design** (04), proof from
**Security Research** (03). Feeds qualified app owners to **GTM** (06).
