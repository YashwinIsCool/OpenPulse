# Positioning

## The one-liner
**Your AI wrote the app. FixProof makes sure nobody can break it — and fixes it
if they can.**

## The category
We are **not** "another pentest tool" or "another scanner." We are the **AI
security engineer for people who don't have one.** The difference is the whole
company: scanners hand you a list of problems; we hand you a **fix that's already
proven to work.**

## Who it's for (and not)
**For:** the builder who shipped a real app using AI coding/app-builder tools, is
technical enough to click "merge," and has **nobody** on security. They feel a
low-grade dread that their app has a hole they can't see.

**Not for (yet):** companies with a security team. They're served by the
enterprise players (XBOW, Horizon3, Pentera, Aikido, Escape). We go where those
tools don't fit: the people too small and too non-expert for them.

## Why we win (positioning pillars)
1. **Fixes, not reports.** The output is a verified fix, not homework. A report
   is useless to someone who can't read it; a merge button isn't.
2. **Proof, not noise.** Every finding comes with a harmless demonstration, so
   there are no false alarms to wade through.
3. **Safe by design.** Human approves every change; nothing auto-deploys; we only
   ever test apps you own and authorize.
4. **Built for non-experts.** Plain English, one clear action, calm not alarmist.

## The feeling we're selling
Not fear — **relief.** "I can finally stop worrying that my app is quietly
broken, because something competent checked it and fixed what it found."

## Proof we'll lead with (as it becomes real)
- The **safe-fix rate** (our one number).
- Before/after demonstrations on **authorized** apps.
- Plain-language teardowns of the kinds of holes AI-built apps commonly ship with.

## One-sentence versions for different rooms
- **To a builder:** "We find the security holes your AI-built app shipped with,
  prove they're real, and send you the fix to approve."
- **To an investor:** "We're the verified-fix layer for the AI-built software
  era — starting with builders who have no security team, aiming to become the
  security step inside every AI app builder."
- **To a skeptic:** "We only test apps you own and authorize, we prove every
  finding harmlessly, and a human approves every fix. Nothing ships on its own."
