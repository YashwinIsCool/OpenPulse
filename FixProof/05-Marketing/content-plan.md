# Content plan

**Purpose in Phase 0:** build just enough credibility that cold outreach gets
replies, and that an app owner who Googles you finds a competent, trustworthy
security voice — not to chase reach. Depth over volume.

## Audience
Builders shipping apps with AI tools. They respond to: specific, concrete,
"here's a real hole and how it happens" content. They distrust fear-bait and
buzzwords.

## Pillars (rotate)
1. **Teardowns:** "The 5 security holes AI-built apps ship with most" — each
   explained plainly, with how to spot and fix it. (Generic education — **never a
   real customer's unfixed app.**)
2. **Proof:** before/after on **authorized** apps, with consent. The fix is the
   hero.
3. **Education:** "What 'user A can see user B's data' actually means, in 60
   seconds." Plain-English security literacy for non-experts.
4. **Build-in-public:** honest progress of building FixProof — the safe-fix rate,
   what's working, what's hard. Founders and builders reward this.

## First 10 pieces (ship order)
1. "Your AI-built app probably has one of these 5 holes" (flagship explainer).
2. "Exposed API keys: the #1 thing AI tools leave in your code."
3. "The scariest bug in AI-built apps: when one user can see another's data."
4. "Why a security *report* is useless if you can't read it — and what to do
   instead."
5. "Is it safe to let a tool test your app? How authorization should work."
6. 60-sec video: "I found a real security hole in an app (with permission) —
   here's the fix."
7. "Scanner vs. security engineer: list of problems vs. solved problems."
8. "Shipped an app with [AI tool]? Here's your 5-minute security self-check."
9. Build-in-public update #1: "We tested 20 AI-built apps. Here's what we found."
   (Aggregate, anonymized, consented.)
10. "How we make sure a fix doesn't break your app" (the safe-fix-rate story).

## Channels (where our user is)
- **X + LinkedIn:** the teardowns and build-in-public. Primary.
- **YouTube Shorts / TikTok:** the 60-second proof and explainers.
- **Reddit / Discord / indie communities:** genuinely helpful answers, not
  spam — link only when relevant.
- **The blog/landing:** home base for the flagship explainers (SEO later).

## Rules
- Every claim backs to something real; cite the **safe-fix rate**, never invented
  numbers.
- Any demonstration uses **authorized, consented** material only.
- Helpful first. We earn the right to pitch by being the clearest security
  explainer in the builder's feed.
