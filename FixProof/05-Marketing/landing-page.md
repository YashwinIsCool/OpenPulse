## The problem (3 cards)
- **AI builds fast, not safe.** AI tools optimize for code that *runs*, not code
  that's *secure*. "It works" and "it's safe" are different tests.
- **The holes are invisible to you.** Exposed API keys, open databases, broken
  logins, "user A can read user B's data" — real, common, and hard to spot
  without security experience.
- **You have no one to call.** Pentests cost thousands. Scanners bury you in
  alerts you can't read. So most builders just… hope.

## How it works (4 steps)
1. **Authorize.** You confirm you own the app and approve exactly what we check.
2. **We find & prove.** FixProof probes for real vulnerabilities and proves each
   one harmlessly — no data touched, nothing taken down.
3. **We fix.** You get a plain-English explanation and a ready-made fix as a pull
   request. You review and merge. We never deploy anything ourselves.
4. **We confirm.** We re-test to prove the hole is actually closed.

## Why FixProof (vs. a scanner)
> A scanner gives you a list of problems. FixProof gives you solved problems.
Every finding is proven, explained in plain English, and comes with a fix you
approve.

## Trust block
- ✅ We only test apps **you own and authorize**, within a scope you set.
- ✅ Demonstrations are **harmless** — we never destroy or copy your users' data,
  never take your app offline.
- ✅ **A human (you) approves every change.** Nothing auto-deploys.
- ✅ Everything is logged. You can withdraw anytime.
