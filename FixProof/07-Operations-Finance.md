# 07 — Operations / Finance

**Mission:** Keep the company alive, legal, and fast — the model, the money, the
entity, the hiring, and the tools — so every other department can move.

## What this department owns
- The financial model and runway.
- Fundraising strategy and materials (when the time comes).
- Legal entity, banking, cap table, basic accounting.
- Hiring plan and the first key roles.
- Internal tooling, vendors, and spend.
- Metrics dashboard (the company's numbers in one place).

## Current priorities (Phase 0)
1. **Keep it lean.** Phase 0 should cost almost nothing but time — no platform
   build, no infra bill, no payroll. Protect runway.
2. **Decide the entity + banking** only when there's a dollar to collect (first
   paying validation customer) — not before.
3. **Draft the simple model:** what a verified fix costs us to deliver vs. what a
   customer pays — the unit economics behind the one metric.
4. **Fundraising readiness, later:** the raise story is "we proved builders pay
   for verified fixes; here's the safe-fix rate; here's the platform path." Don't
   pitch before the gate is cleared.

## First roles to hire (post-validation, rough order)
1. Founding security engineer (owns the method with Research).
2. Founding product engineer (owns the platform + GitHub loop).
3. Design-minded full-stack (owns the non-expert UX).
Everything before that is founder-run.

## The money question that matters
Small builders pay little per customer. Ops/Finance keeps Executive honest on the
math: either **volume** (free-score funnel → platform distribution) or **upmarket
later** must plausibly cover cost. If neither pencils even at a great safe-fix
rate, that's a strategy signal, not a spreadsheet detail.

## Docs to create here
- `financial-model.md` · `fundraising.md` · `hiring-plan.md` · `metrics.md`

## How it connects
Serves every department with runway + tooling. Gets unit-cost inputs from
**Engineering** (02) and revenue signal from **GTM** (06). Reports the company
numbers to **Executive** (00). Works with **Legal/Compliance** (08) on entity.
