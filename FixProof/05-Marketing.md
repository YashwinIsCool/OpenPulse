# 05 — Marketing

**Mission:** Reach builders shipping AI-made apps, name the fear they already
feel, and convert it into a free security score — then into a paying customer.

## What this department owns
- Brand voice and positioning (with Design on identity).
- The **free-score funnel**: the hook that gets people in the door.
- Content: the demonstrations, teardowns, and plain-English security education
  that build authority with non-expert builders.
- Channels where our user actually is.
- Launch narrative.

## Positioning
- **One line:** "Your AI wrote the app. FixProof makes sure nobody can break
  it — and fixes it if they can."
- **Category:** not "another pentest tool." We're the **AI security engineer**
  for people who don't have one.
- **Proof, not fear:** the free score opens the door; the **verified fix** is the
  product. We never sell on panic — the demonstrated fix is the credibility.

## Where our user is
Indie-hacker / AI-builder communities, the AI app-builder ecosystems and their
showcases, X/LinkedIn builder audiences, YouTube/TikTok "I built an app with AI"
creators, relevant subreddits and Discords. (This workspace has social/content
tooling connected — Marketing can draft and schedule from here.)

## Current priorities (Phase 0)
Marketing is in **support of validation**, not growth yet:
1. Write the **cold outreach** that gets 20 app owners to agree to an authorized
   test (hands off to GTM, dept 06).
2. Draft the **one-pager / landing** explaining the free score → verified fix.
3. Line up **1–2 credible demonstrations** (on authorized apps only) as the first
   content proof — coordinate with Security Research (03).
4. Reserve the name/handles once Executive confirms the brand.
