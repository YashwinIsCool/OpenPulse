Why it's different

Most heart apps show you a number. This one shows you yourself.

A number you forget by lunch isn't a habit. StethoAI is built around the two things that actually bring you back.

30-second check

Your camera is the sensor

Rest a fingertip on the camera. StethoAI reads your pulse from the light passing through your skin — the same principle as a pulse oximeter. No watch, no band, nothing to buy.

Learns your heart

Patterns, not just numbers

Check in each day — sleep, stress, caffeine, mood. StethoAI connects the dots and tells you what actually moves your heart rate: “Your resting rate runs higher after short sleep.”

Calm on command

Watch your heart settle

One guided flow: a reading, a breath, another reading. See your heart rate drop in real time — proof you can steady yourself in about two minutes.
