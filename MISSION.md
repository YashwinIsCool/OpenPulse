# StethoAI — Mission

## Mission

**Put a basic heart check in the hands of people who have no other way to get one — and connect them to care that already exists.**

StethoAI turns an ordinary phone camera into a free heart-awareness tool, built first
for underserved communities where wearables, screenings, and doctors are out of reach.

## Who we serve

Not the person who already owns an Apple Watch, a gym membership, and a cardiologist.

**The person who owns none of those.** Students, low-income families, elderly people on
fixed incomes, and communities — beginning in underserved parts of India — where
cardiovascular disease is common, under-screened, and often caught far too late.

## The problem

Cardiovascular disease is the number one cause of death in the world, and it is
**dramatically under-diagnosed in underserved populations.** In India specifically, high
blood pressure, diabetes, and rheumatic heart disease are widespread, while access to
screening and follow-up is not. People who cannot afford a wearable or a clinic visit
often have *no* window into their own heart health until something goes badly wrong.

A tool that needs nothing but a phone someone may already have is not a nice-to-have for
this audience. It is access where there was none.

## What StethoAI honestly does — and does not do

**Does:**
- Measures heart **rate** from the phone camera (photoplethysmography)
- Surfaces **rhythm awareness** — flagging an irregular or out-of-range heartbeat worth a
  professional's attention
- Keeps a **portable heart record** a person can carry to a doctor, where records rarely exist
- Provides **education** in plain, local language
- Connects a flagged person to **real, local pathways to care**

**Does NOT:**
- Diagnose any condition
- Measure blood pressure or blood sugar (no hardware for it — these need a cuff and a test)
- Replace a clinician

Overclaiming in a community with limited medical access is not a marketing sin — it is a
way to cause harm. We say exactly what the tool can and cannot do, every time.

## Our commitments (non-negotiable)

1. **Never screen without a pathway to care.** We do not run a screening until we have
   arranged where flagged people can actually go. Screening with nowhere to refer is harm.
2. **Awareness, not diagnosis.** Every result points toward a clinician, never away from one.
3. **Free for those who need it.** The mission is help, not revenue.
4. **Do no harm — accuracy and honesty first.** No false reassurance. If we're not sure, we say so.
5. **Dignity.** A referral is given with respect, not alarm.
6. **Built with clinicians, not alone.** Deployed only in partnership with a cardiologist and
   a real referral network (health camps, PHCs, partner hospitals, community health workers).

## Direction (mentor- and pilot-driven)

The biggest work ahead is not flashy: **reading accuracy and validation, local language and
low-literacy design, a built-in pathway-to-care, and a doctor-ready record.** Likely next:
**rhythm/irregular-beat detection** and eventually **Android** (the phones this audience
actually uses). A longer-term, high-potential frontier is **heart-sound screening for
rheumatic heart disease**, which is prevalent in underserved Indian communities — pending
solving hard audio and validation problems.

We do not build these blind. A clinical partner and a real first pilot decide what matters.
